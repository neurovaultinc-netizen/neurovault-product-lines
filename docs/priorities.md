# Product priorities for NeuroVault

## Executive summary

NeuroVault should be built in a deliberate sequence that moves from understanding to prediction to action.

The product family should follow this order:

1. NeuroVault Insight
2. NeuroVault Forecast
3. NeuroVault Studio
4. NeuroVault Copilot

## Priority 1: NeuroVault Insight

This is the most important initial priority.

### Why it comes first

- It creates the strongest immediate customer value
- It makes the core NeuroVault promise feel real: systems that detect patterns and explain them
- It is easier to explain and easier to sell as a focused SaaS product
- It establishes trust before adding predictive or autonomous functionality

### Strategic goal

Help end users understand what is happening in their data in real time.

### Core outcomes

- identify meaningful patterns
- detect anomalies early
- explain the signal clearly
- reduce time spent manually analyzing data

## Priority 2: NeuroVault Forecast

Once the pattern-recognition product proves value, the next product should add predictive capability.

### Why it comes second

- users want to know what is likely to happen next
- the brand becomes more powerful with prediction built in
- it naturally extends the insight layer into planning and decision support

### Strategic goal

Help end users anticipate likely future outcomes based on present and historical signals.

### Core outcomes

- forecast behavior and trends
- surface risk and opportunity
- support planning and scenario thinking
- provide confidence in future-oriented actions

## Priority 3: NeuroVault Studio

This product establishes the system-building layer.

### Why it comes third

- users need ways to shape workflows and pipelines
- the platform needs a configuration layer to support advanced use cases
- it gives the portfolio a more complete operating model

### Strategic goal

Offer a structured environment for building and tuning intelligence workflows.

### Core outcomes

- connect data sources
- define analysis logic
- configure segmentation and conditions
- support advanced operators and teams

## Priority 4: NeuroVault Copilot

This product sits at the top of the portfolio and supports action.

### Why it comes fourth

- it depends on the earlier products working well
- it adds guidance and decision support rather than replacing the core product value
- it creates a premium user experience for teams that want help acting on insight

### Strategic goal

Turn insight and prediction into guided action.

### Core outcomes

- answer user questions in context
- summarize patterns and recommendations
- provide next-step guidance
- support decision-making without requiring a deep technical workflow

## Recommended sequencing

### Phase 1: Foundation
- Define NeuroVault Insight as the flagship product
- Validate core pattern-recognition use cases
- Build trust and product-market fit

### Phase 2: Prediction layer
- Add predictive intelligence and forecasting
- Position the platform as more than analysis
- Expand toward proactive decision support

### Phase 3: Builder layer
- Add studio capabilities for configuration and workflow design
- Let advanced users customize the platform

### Phase 4: Action layer
- Add AI-powered guidance and recommendations
- Turn insight into operational action

## Final recommendation

The portfolio should be built around a clear progression:

- Understand the world
- Predict what is likely next
- Configure the system around the user
- Act with support and guidance

That is the core sequence of a product line built for end users who want systems that think.
